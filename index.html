Bermuda Buddy

Lawn care for Bermuda grass, one week at a time.

Privacy Support

About

Bermuda Buddy tells you what your Bermuda lawn needs this week, such as mowing, watering or fertilizing, and gives you a one-line reason for each task. It uses your local soil temperature and forecast. There are no accounts, no ads and no store.

Privacy Policy

Effective: [publish date]

Bermuda Buddy keeps your lawn info on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we don't sell or share your data.

On your phone only. Your lawn size, grass type, mower settings and activity log never leave your phone. If you delete the app, they're deleted too.

Weather. To get your soil temperature and forecast, the app sends your location to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a free weather service. The location is rounded to about 1 km first, and nothing else goes with it. The app doesn't keep a record of your location.

ZIP codes. If you type a ZIP code instead of using GPS, Apple's built-in maps service turns it into a location on your phone's behalf.

Feature requests. When you tap Send, your idea, your email if you added one, and your app version, iOS version and device model go to us through Formspree (formspree.io), a form service. We use them only to read your idea and reply. Leave the email blank if you don't want a reply.

Children. Bermuda Buddy isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes. If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Support

Have a question or found a bug? Email alexbeard@mail.grokbot.com, or open the app and go to Settings > Request a Feature.

Common questions

  • Why isn't fertilizer showing up? The app only recommends fertilizer once the soil is warm enough, your lawn is fully green, and you've logged two mows. If soil data is unavailable, you can enter a probe reading.
  • Where is my data? Only on your phone. The app has no account or cloud backup in this version.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. It shows your last saved plan and when it was last updated.
